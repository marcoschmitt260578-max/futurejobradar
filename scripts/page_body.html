Why this exists

Not a forecast. An invitation.

Every week someone says the machines are coming for our jobs. Some tasks will go, that's true. But every big shift in history also created work nobody could imagine before it happened.

Printing press → editors, publishers, booksellers
Electricity → electricians, engineers, radio hosts
Internet → web designers, app developers, Uber drivers, Airbnb hosts, influencers
AI, robots, space, biotech → ?

This radar is my attempt to fill in that question mark. I read what scientists, analysts and futurists say, mix it with the best science fiction, and ask one simple thing: what new jobs could this create?

Some of these jobs will happen. Some won't. Some will look completely different. That's fine. The point is not to be right. The point is to look at the future with curiosity instead of fear, because every change, however big, also opens doors.
