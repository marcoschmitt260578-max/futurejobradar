Job of the week · arrives ~2032

Orbital Dark-Sky Mediator

When thousands of sunlit data-centre satellites share the dawn-dusk orbit, observatories, operators and regulators need someone to agree on brightness and timing.

Negotiates brightness limits, orbit slots and observing windows between satellite operators, observatories and dark-sky communities. Turns astronomers' sky models into contracts that operators can actually meet.

A day on the job: She spends the morning running a sky-brightness simulation for a new constellation, then brokers a deal in which the operator tilts its panels during an observatory's survey week.
