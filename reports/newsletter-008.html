Job of the week · arrives ~2030

Memory Cue Designer

When home robots and AI callers help people with early memory loss every day, someone must decide which words, photos, sounds and moments they use.

Designs the daily set of prompts, objects and conversation styles that a care robot or AI companion uses with one person. Builds them from that person's life story with family and nurses, then tests and adjusts them.

A day on the job: She spends the morning with a retired bus driver's daughter choosing the three songs and two photos the home robot offers when he gets restless at 4 p.m., then reviews last week's logs to see which cue calmed him.
