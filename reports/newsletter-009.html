Job of the week · arrives ~2030

Soil Memorial Steward

When human composting is legal across more states and countries, families receive soil and need someone to help decide where it goes and look after it.

Guides families on where the soil from natural organic reduction ends up: a memorial grove, a garden or a shared forest. Tracks soil quality, local rules and the growing memorial over many years.

A day on the job: Check the soil batch from Tuesday's vessel, walk a family through three tree-planting spots, then review the grove's sensor map for the trees planted last spring.
