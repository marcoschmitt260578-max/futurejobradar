Job of the week · arrives ~2029

Master Trade Recorder

When master electricians and welders retire faster than apprentices arrive, the robots that replace missing hands need teachers who capture how the work is really done.

Follows veteran tradespeople on site with cameras and sensors, asks why they work the way they do, and turns that know-how into training data for robots and lessons for apprentices.

A day on the job: Morning filming a veteran electrician pulling cable through a tight data hall, afternoon tagging the moments where she changes her grip, then running the clips past a robot trainee.
