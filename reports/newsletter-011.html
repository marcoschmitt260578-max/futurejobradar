Job of the week · arrives ~2030

Rental Robot Reset Technician

When robots are rented by the month or the hour, every return must leave the machine safe, clean and free of the last customer's data.

Between customers, resets a rented humanoid. Wipes home maps, voices and learned habits, tests joints and cameras, and certifies the unit for the next household or site.

A day on the job: She scans a returned robot, finds a stranger's kitchen layout still in its memory, wipes it, runs a grip test and signs the unit off for a noon handover.
