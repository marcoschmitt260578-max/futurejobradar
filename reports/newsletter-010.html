Job of the week · arrives ~2030

Practice-Home Set Dresser

When thousands of humanoids must learn chores by practising, someone has to build homes worth practising in.

Designs and stages deliberately lived-in practice houses, kitchens and care rooms so robots meet the clutter, odd objects and surprises of real life. Works like a film set dresser, but the audience is a robot learning.

A day on the job: Before dawn she restages forty kitchens with a wobbly chair, a sticky drawer and toys left on the stairs, then reads which mess tripped the robots.
